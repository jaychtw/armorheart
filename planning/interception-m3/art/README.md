The two selected PNG masters under `source/` are preserved without re-encoding; no poster pixels, named historical vessel copy, flags, labels or national insignia were extracted into this sample. No stock purchase/new paid account/API service or runtime package was introduced. Underlying source-reference authorship/license remain unverified; no blanket production/redistribution rights are asserted.

The SVG is a **mixed raster/vector editable composition**, not a vector-redrawn vessel or recovered historical layered source. Named layers expose exact hull silhouette, painted deck/superstructure underlay, hull edge, reusable painted gunhouse and original vector primary bore, three fixed-starboard battery instances and a hidden registration guide. Deck/architecture and gunhouse textures are raster; they are not separate editable individual bolts/funnels. Underpainting registration is an explicit nonuniform SVG transform; original pixels stay unchanged. The single primary bore per battery gives exactly three visible firing mouths matching the three-shell working default; it does not add a nine-shell multi-barrel attack.

Body footprint remains the accepted320×88 polygon; mount centers−88/0/+88 and actual primary mouths atlocalY66 remain unchanged. Barrels are layered below the gunhouse to conceal the inboard mounting portion. The accepted Guardian is shown with its exact assets/pivot/.5 scale, barrel beneath hull. Boat and turret assembly rotate together through the unchanged curved route; no independent gun aiming, fog transition or snap heading.

Historical reference hulls are roughly7–8:1, versus the working3.64:1. This compact stylization deliberately preserves current geometry/readability. A literal slender historical hull would need a material beam/target change (about40–46 wide at320 length); it has not been silently substituted. The fictional equal-spaced three-primary-battery layout is a readable gameplay adaptation, not a claim to reproduce Iowa/Yamato or their historical bore counts. These identity choices remain for creative review; speed/route/volley timings and the12.72s balance consideration are not retuned.
