# Fortress — original editable M4 artwork

Owner accepted the Fortress identity/direction at Offer `a44485475d573d65228d70e377e2561fed9f0e47` and authorized material/lighting refinement at `ce298580273e34da872f1d99e5150edbcc109c12`. This bounded implementation refines that original warm-stone/olive bastion; final composed M4 creative acceptance is pending.

`fortress-source.svg` contains editable Intact, Broken and Half-destroyed construction with deterministic vector stone wear, bevel light/shadow and collapsed roof masses. The approved study under `planning/siege-m4/art/` is unchanged. No external concept pixels, paid asset/service, stock fortress or photoreal master is used. `tools/export_fortress.py` reproduces the refined master and three512×320 body PNGs using Chromium154.0.8037.57. Registration/source/output hashes are in `anchors.json`.

Every body uses the same visibly solid256×160 foundation at `(256,64)`, including damaged states. The three roof pivots stay `(288,216)`, `(384,216)`, `(480,216)`. `barrel.svg` is a separate24-unit gun layer; `gun-cap.svg` covers its mounting portion. The projectile origin and flash match its actual rotated tip. Damage silhouettes change roof masses, not physics or the five-shot ability. Decorative smoke is nonphysical and restrained under reduced motion.

Guardian comparison uses accepted project assets without modification. Existing project licensing/provenance caveats remain; this file does not grant rights over underlying Guardian art or assert blanket production redistribution clearance. No new licensing claims, insignia or historical references are introduced.
