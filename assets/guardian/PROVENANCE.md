# Guardian prototype provenance

The owner supplied `Armorheart_GDD_and_Offer_v1.zip`, containing `tank_prototype_v01.zip`. `original-master.png` is the unmodified T01 Guardian 256px RGBA master from that pack. Its README describes AI-generated concept-sheet cutouts, not production-approved art, and supplies no explicit production redistribution license.

Only Guardian is reconstructed. Editable SVG masks separate the supplied barrel pixels from the fixed hull/turret; a small vector repair beneath the barrel supplies the surface exposed by recoil. No paid tools, stock art, fonts or external services are used. No additional tank is reconstructed. The asset is a mixed raster/vector prototype, not a fully vector-redrawn tank or recovered original layered artwork.
