# Colossus M2 — review build

Extract the ZIP and open `guardian.html`. If your browser restricts local files, serve the extracted folder with `python3 -m http.server 8080 --bind 127.0.0.1`, then open `http://127.0.0.1:8080/guardian.html`. This self-contained file needs no external requests after loading. Direct double-click/file opening was not verified in managed Chromium.

Choose **Colossus · Level 2**, then Start mission. Arrows/WASD move; hold Space to fire. On touch, hold a direction and Fire together. Guardian faces immediately and fires once per second. The original range and Breakthrough Level 1 remain available.

Colossus has four HP; Guardian has three. Shoot the visible grounded body. Airborne shots pass underneath. Follow the marked next landing and move clear of grounded contact. A hit grants Guardian three seconds of protection while movement and shooting remain available. There is no mission timer. Defeat Colossus to win; losing all tank HP ends the attempt.

Pause, confirmed retry and level exit are available. Switch English/繁體中文 live; mute and reduced motion controls are beside Pause. Sound starts muted. On short landscape screens, scroll to reach controls.

Known balance issue: an informed player can drive to a safe firing lane and hold Fire to win in roughly 7.4–7.6 seconds without further movement or damage. The approved HP, sequence and timing were retained for creative review. This is a bounded review build, pending final Confirm & Close, not a public release. Physical-phone and independent first-time-player tests were not performed.

繁體中文：選擇「巨獸・第二關」並開始。方向鍵或 WASD 移動，空白鍵開火；觸控可同時按住方向與「開火」。巨獸落地時才能命中，跳躍時砲彈從下方穿過。守護者受傷後有三秒保護，仍可移動及開火。本關沒有倒數計時，可暫停、重試或離開。
