768×768 world, bounds0..768 on both axes, **no wall cells**. Walls are optional under the GDD; omit them in the first boss mission to preserve readable landing/evasion space and avoid adding a demolition puzzle. No wall-crushing or new attack system is needed in this offer; existing range/R3 one-hit walls and steel remain intact.

No random tracking, shockwave, extra enemy or HP-driven speed/attack changes.

Final authored poses must fit the same contract or be reassessed. The art-only slider is registration evidence, not a completed jump animation.

**Proposed interaction defaults, still awaiting approval as part of this Offer:**

- Boss grounded throughout each2s pause; airborne throughout transit. Grounded shots deal1 damage; airborne shots pass underneath and do not consume themselves against a fictitious ground target. Clearly show airborne/grounded status. Vulnerability is the whole grounded interval, not a hidden short weak-point window.
- Projectile hurt geometry follows the **visible projected grounded body core**, with transparent gaps removed, authored from accepted views. It is distinct from the floor-contact ellipse. Exact body proxies are an art-gate deliverable; the full sprite rectangle is not automatically a target and the elevated chest/head cannot silently become unhittable because only feet were tested.
- Provisional floor contact ellipse radii112×64. Validate against all final knuckles/feet and planted mass; the actual contact footprint must match the telegraph. Only grounded/landing body contact causes1 damage; no in-flight corridor damage, shockwave or wider unseen radius. Contact is damaging but nonblocking, without push/teleport/knockback, so the tank can escape rather than be trapped. Sort by ground anchor and keep the tank identifiable under tall-body overhang with a restrained tank silhouette cue if needed; this is M2 presentation only, without a bounding box or physics change. These are conservative proposals where the GDD leaves boundaries open.
- Damage grants exactly3s of immunity to **all defined damage**, with local hull flash/status, while movement/fire remain usable. Immunity and boss phases share Guardian's fixed simulation clock; pause/hidden freezes both. A long visible frame must not advance the boss through unseen real-time jumps while tank physics is capped. R3's separate real-active90s deadline is unchanged.
- Boss HP4/3–2/1/0 maps to Intact/Fatigued/Wounded/Defeated immediately. Appearance changes posture/guard/scuff detail plus localized HP label, without unapproved mechanical phases. Tank0 loses; boss0 wins. Resolve impacts/contact chronologically; propose player-safe victory for an exact simultaneous final-shot/contact tie, suppressing contact from the newly defeated boss. Earlier lethal contact remains a loss. No post-terminal damage or late win.

## Fixed-turret feasibility and fairness

[Calculations](../../planning/colossus-m2/evidence/assessment.json) use accepted hull/movement and the proposed schedule, **not implemented boss combat**. From spawn, drive right along y704 to(560,704):496/156=3.179s. Wait for LR, face up, emit at6.45/7.45s. From that station to the current visible opaque torso point, conservative shell travel is0.375s at480 units/s; both arrivals precede takeoff8.4s. Leave after7.5s, move left352 units to(208,704), face up; ready by9.773s. Emit9.85/10.85s; arrivals10.225/11.225 precede LL takeoff11.6s. One-second cadence is respected; no aim rotation or movement change is needed. Final views must preserve a hittable cardinal body core for this witness; production swept-collision/playthrough must reverify it.

These firing stations leave50.5 units between the accepted north-facing hull and the proposed contact footprint. The empty arena permits escaping a destination footprint in under1s in a free cardinal direction; the2s telegraph plus1.2s flight gives margin. Spawn's northward ray misses every grounded nominal sprite frame by≥16.4 units, avoiding an immediate stationary spawn-fire solution. **Safe camping remains a concrete balance tradeoff:** after moving to(560,704), the tank can stay clear of all five grounded contact footprints. Proposed shots3.25/4.25 can hit UR by4.158/5.158, followed by LR hits6.825/7.825 without further movement. A knowledgeable player can therefore win in about7.8s using a safe lane. The fixed loop, grounded vulnerability, unlimited ammo and no deadline allow patient camping; an empty arena does not force dodging. This Offer proposes keeping the simple learnable boss and playtesting that tradeoff, **not quietly adding retargeting/attacks, mandatory targets, tighter vulnerability, more HP or a deadline**. Owner authorization must include acceptance of this characteristic; if continuous forced evasion is essential, revise the combat design explicitly before implementation.
